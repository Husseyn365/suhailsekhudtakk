Ground Reality

— Ek Hum-Safar (Real Ground Observation)

12 Saal Tech Aur AI Ki Bheed: Subah Hustle, Raat Ko Existential Crisis

Pichhle 12 saalon se main E-Commerce, emerging AI technologies aur consulting ke ecosystem me kaam kar raha hoon. Main jhanklo.com aur The Slicemasters ka Co-founder raha hoon. Maine zero se multi-crore brands scale kiye hain, hazaron PPTs banayi hain, targets chase kiye hain.

Lekin is tech rat-race me maine ek cheez note ki: Bahar se sabka LinkedIn profile चमक raha hai, par andar se sabki mental battery 2% par hai aur charger kho gaya hai!

Hum sab imposter syndrome, toxic hustle culture, aur 'Sharma ji ka beta kitna kama raha hai' ke chakkar me apne hi dimag ka kabada kar baithe hain.

LifeBridge Movement: Jab Zindagi Ne Thappad Maar Kar Jagaya

Mujhe fakhr hai ki main LifeBridge movement ka ek hissa raha hoon — ek aisi tehreek jise hamare senior mentors aur inspirational authors ne shuru kiya.

Wahan se maine insani jazbaat, gir kar uthne ka asli stamina, aur psychological resilience seekha. Maine dekha ki jab ek insaan doosre insaan se bina kisi judge kiye sachhi baat karta hai, toh bade se bada depression aur khauf pighal jata hai.

Psychology Ko Standup Style Me Kyun Samjha Raha Hoon?

Kyunki bhai, clinical textbooks itni boring hoti hain ki unhe padh kar aadha banda waise hi so jaye ya confuse ho jaye!

Jab hum bolte hain 'Catastrophic Overthinking', toh aadha log ghabra jate hain. Par jab main bolta hoon 'Bhai raat ko 2 baje jo tere dimag me bina matlab ka kalesh chal raha hai', toh foran samajh me aata hai!

Fight-or-Flight Mode

Dimag ko lagta hai jungle ka Babbar Sher aa gaya hai, jabki asal me bas credit card bill ka SMS aaya hai!

Cognitive Distortions

Apne hi dimag ke banaye hue fake news WhatsApp forwards aur 3 AM conspiracy theories par andha vishwas karna.

People Pleasing

Desi rishtedaaron aur 'Log kya kahenge' ko khush karne ke chakkar me apni jawani aur mental health barbaad karna.
