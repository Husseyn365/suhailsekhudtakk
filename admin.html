10 Golden Rules to Scale in the AI Era

The proven checklist developed across 12 years of enterprise and startup e-commerce management.

01

Ruthless Niche Focus

Do not be an Amazon. Pick one hyper-specific painful problem for a clearly defined audience. When you try to sell to everybody, you sell to nobody.

02

High-Converting Frictionless Checkout

Enable one-click express checkouts (Shop Pay, Apple Pay, Google Pay). 70% of carts are abandoned due to tedious address forms.

03

Leverage AI for Catalog Speed

Use generative models for bulk product descriptions, SEO meta tags, and high-fashion product backgrounds instead of expensive studio shoots.

04

Obsess Over Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)

First order ad cost can break even. Real wealth is created in repeat automated retention via WhatsApp & email journeys.

05

Protect Your Founder Mental Health

The business cannot outgrow the emotional capacity of its founder. Practice mindfulness and disconnect daily to prevent costly burnout.
